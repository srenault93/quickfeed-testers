About QuickFeed

Every story, from every angle.

QuickFeed aggregates coverage from multiple outlets, then uses AI to produce a short, balanced summary of each story — with sentiment and source diversity built in.

Multi-source summaries

Each brief pulls from several outlets so you get the full picture, not a single slant.

Sentiment at a glance

Every story is tagged positive, negative, neutral, or mixed so tone never catches you off guard.

Topics you care about

Choose your interests and QuickFeed tailors the daily brief to what actually matters to you.

Built for speed

Key points, not walls of text. Read the brief in under a minute, or tap through for depth.

How testing works

Three steps to get started

You'll join our tester list, opt in on Google Play, and start receiving the app like any other Play Store install.

Join the group

Click the button below to join the QuickFeed testers Google Group. This adds your Google account to our closed-test roster.

Opt in on Google Play

Once you're in the group, you'll get a Play Store opt-in link. Tap it on your Android phone to become a tester.

Install & explore

QuickFeed will appear in the Play Store for your account. Install, use it, and send us your thoughts — every bit of feedback helps.
